Our Services

The company works in six main areas and offers clients complete services from supply, shipping and transport through to execution and handover.

01

Vehicle Trading

The company imports and sells new and used vehicles from the Gulf, the United States, South Korea and China, and handles every step until the vehicle is delivered to the client ready for use.

  • Vehicle import to client specification
  • Sale of new and used vehicles
  • Technical inspection and vehicle history reports
  • Fleet supply for companies and government departments
  • Genuine spare parts
  • Registration and licensing procedures

02

International Shipping

The company provides sea and air freight services from around the world to Iraq in cooperation with accredited shipping lines and freight agents, and handles customs clearance, insurance and shipment tracking until delivery.

  • Sea freight, full (FCL) and shared (LCL) containers
  • Air freight for cargo and parcels
  • Customs clearance at ports and border crossings
  • Cargo insurance
  • Shipment tracking with regular client updates
  • Import services on behalf of companies

03

Land Transport

The company owns a fleet of trucks and specialised vehicles for moving goods and equipment between Iraqi governorates and to neighbouring countries, in line with safety standards and agreed schedules. All trucks are fitted with tracking systems.

  • General cargo and container transport
  • Refrigerated transport for food and medical goods
  • Heavy equipment and machinery transport
  • International road transit
  • Storage and warehousing
  • Recurring transport contracts for companies and factories

Construction & Contracting

The company carries out building and civil contracting works to approved drawings and specifications under specialised engineering supervision, from excavation and foundations through final finishing and project handover.

  • Residential and commercial buildings and complexes
  • Industrial facilities and steel structures
  • Infrastructure and road works
  • Electrical and mechanical works
  • Interior and exterior finishing
  • Renovation and maintenance

05

Solar Energy

The company studies, designs, supplies and installs solar energy systems of all types and capacities for homes, commercial and industrial facilities and agricultural projects, using high-quality components from accredited manufacturers.

  • Hybrid, off-grid and on-grid systems
  • Solar water pumping for agriculture
  • Commercial and industrial solar plants
  • Solar street lighting and water heaters
  • Site survey and load calculation
  • Scheduled maintenance and monitoring

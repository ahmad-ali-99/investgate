Solar Energy

Study, design, supply and installation of solar energy systems of all types and capacities, with warranty and maintenance services.

Solar Energy Division

Clean, reliable energy for homes and facilities

The company’s solar division delivers solar energy systems for residential, commercial, industrial and agricultural clients. Each project starts with a site survey and an accurate load calculation, followed by the design of a suitable system and component selection matched to the client’s needs and budget.

We work with accredited manufacturers of panels, inverters and batteries, and provide a written warranty on components and installation, together with scheduled maintenance contracts.

  • Free site survey
  • Components from accredited manufacturers
  • Trained technical team
  • Staged payment available

System Types

The Solar Systems We Install

We select the right system type based on the intended use, availability of the national grid and the required operating hours.

Hybrid System

Runs on solar power during the day, stores the surplus in batteries and draws on the national grid when available. The best choice where power cuts are frequent.

Suitable for: Homes, shops, offices

Off-Grid System

Operates completely independently of the national grid, relying on solar panels and lithium batteries to provide power around the clock.

Suitable for: Farms, remote sites, telecom towers

On-Grid System

Connects directly to the national grid without batteries, reducing electricity and fuel consumption during daylight hours at a lower cost.

Suitable for: Factories, commercial buildings, government offices

Solar Water Pumping

Runs submersible and surface pumps directly on solar power for irrigation, without fuel or batteries.

Suitable for: Orchards, fields, artesian wells

Commercial & Industrial Plants

Ground-mounted or rooftop plants from 100 kW upwards, with an economic feasibility study and complete engineering design.

Suitable for: Factories, complexes, investment projects

Solar Lighting & Water Heaters

Integrated solar street lights for roads, complexes and parks, and solar water heaters for homes, hotels and hospitals.

Suitable for: Municipalities, residential complexes, hotels

02

Galvanised steel or aluminium mounting structures, with breakers, protection devices and compliant cabling.

Solar Energy

Solar energy systems designed around your actual needs

We carry out a site survey and an accurate load calculation before designing each system, and supply components from accredited manufacturers with a written warranty and scheduled maintenance.

System Details
Drag or scroll to explore the systems
01

Hybrid System

Runs on solar power during the day, stores the surplus in batteries and draws on the national grid when available. The best choice where power cuts are frequent.

02

Off-Grid System

Operates completely independently of the national grid, relying on solar panels and lithium batteries to provide power around the clock.

03

On-Grid System

Connects directly to the national grid without batteries, reducing electricity and fuel consumption during daylight hours at a lower cost.

04

Solar Water Pumping

Runs submersible and surface pumps directly on solar power for irrigation, without fuel or batteries.

05

Commercial & Industrial Plants

Ground-mounted or rooftop plants from 100 kW upwards, with an economic feasibility study and complete engineering design.

06

Solar Lighting & Water Heaters

Integrated solar street lights for roads, complexes and parks, and solar water heaters for homes, hotels and hospitals.

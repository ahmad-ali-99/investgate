About Us

Learn about Invest Gate General Trading & Contracting, its vision, mission and the values it works by.

Company Profile

Invest Gate General Trading & Contracting

2012

Invest Gate is a private Iraqi company founded in Baghdad in 2012 and registered with the Companies Registration Office of the Ministry of Trade. It began in vehicle trading and customs clearance and, as its clients’ needs grew, gradually expanded into international shipping, land transport, construction contracting, general trading and supply, and solar energy systems.

Today the company serves individuals, private companies and government entities across Iraq. It relies on qualified engineering, technical and administrative staff, a company-owned transport fleet, warehouses in Baghdad and Basra, and a network of agents and suppliers in the Gulf states, Turkey and China.

  • Head office in Baghdad
  • Branch and warehouses in Basra
  • Contractor classification certificate
  • Member of the Baghdad Chamber of Commerce

Vision & Mission

Our Direction

Our Vision

To be one of Iraq’s leading companies in trading, contracting and renewable energy, and a trusted partner to our clients in delivering their projects.

Our Mission

To provide integrated, high-quality services at competitive prices, to meet agreed technical specifications and schedules, and to contribute to economic development and the transition to clean energy.

Our Objectives

  • Extend our services to cover all governorates.
  • Raise staff capability and improve working methods.
  • Build partnerships with international manufacturers and suppliers.
  • Help expand the use of solar energy.

Quality & Safety

Quality, Health and Safety Policy

The company applies a quality control system that includes inspecting materials before supply, continuous engineering supervision on site, and final inspection before handover. Occupational health and safety procedures are applied on all work sites and transport operations.

  • Inspection of materials and components against specifications before use
  • Personal protective equipment and training for all workers
  • Scheduled maintenance of the transport fleet and equipment
  • Documentation of work stages and regular client reports
